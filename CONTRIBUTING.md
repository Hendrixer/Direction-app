# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help.

**Issues are welcome.** If something's broken or confusing, [open an issue](https://github.com/Hendrixer/Direction-app/issues) with what you did, what you expected and what happened. Direction's log (`~/Library/Logs/Direction/Direction.log`) and a crash report, if there is one, help a lot. Leave out anything private from your films.

**Pull requests from outside can't be accepted yet.** Direction is [fair source](LICENSE.md) and its signed app is sold, so every contribution needs a contributor agreement first, and there isn't one yet. When there is, this page will say how to sign it. Until then, an issue that describes the fix is the best way to send one.

**You're free to change your own copy.** The license lets you build and change Direction for your own use (see [the README](README.md#license-and-paying-for-direction)).
