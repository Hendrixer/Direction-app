Some films got stuck in the Cut room with every note accepted: **Lock picture…** was greyed out with no reason, and Finish stayed closed. The cause was an unasked change, something the editor did on its own in an earlier cut, that was never accepted before the next turnover. Once a newer cut came, the sheet stopped showing that change, but it still counted as an open note, so nothing could unlock the button.

Now a change the editor made on its own counts only while it waits in the cut you're looking at. Once a newer cut is made from it, it's part of the edit. If you're stuck on an older version, update and **Lock picture…** works on the cut you have.

**Lock picture…** also never sits greyed out anymore. It always opens its sheet, and the sheet lists anything still in the way, each with the button that fixes it.
