title: Styles stay out of your films

**Making a style no longer adds a film.** A style made with no film open used to show up in Films as a film called "Styles". It doesn't anymore, and if one is there from 0.3.11, it goes away when you update. Your styles are where they belong, in Settings › Styles.

**The editor studies a video for a style without stopping to ask.** With Codex as the editor, making a style could stop on a question about whether it may read what Direction measured in the video. Every editor now reads and writes the files in its own working folder on its own, and only asks you about the style itself.
