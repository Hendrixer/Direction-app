Once you lock picture, the edit is final. The Cut room now says so: the top of the notes reads **Picture is locked**, explains that color, sound, captions and delivery happen in Finish, and offers **Unlock Picture…** if you want a change to the edit. The steel button takes you to Finish.

Before, you could keep writing notes on a locked cut, and they went nowhere. Now the tools for making a note step aside while picture is locked, and pressing N tells you why instead of making a note that can't be sent. The cut still plays, so you can watch it through once more before you deliver.

### Make a style from a video in Settings

**Settings › Styles › New Style** now starts with **From a video**. Paste a YouTube link (or choose a file), say what you like about it, and press Make Style. **Write it yourself** is one click away.

While the style is being made, it's the first row in Your styles, saying what's happening: downloading the video, studying it, or the editor writing it. If something goes wrong, the row says what, with **Try Again**.

## Fixes

- **Locking scenes after an assembly** no longer stops with "Scene not found". Shots from takes that aren't in a scene lock with the picture, and a cut with no scenes offers just Lock picture.
- **Styles from YouTube videos** open on every Mac. Direction now asks for a version of the video that macOS always plays, where some downloads used to fail with "Cannot Open".
- **No more pause after a video downloads.** Direction used to stop for a few seconds while it read what the video page said about it.
- **A crash when a popover changed size** while it was open is fixed.
- **Looking at footage on this Mac** says when mlx-vlm needs an update, with the command to copy, instead of failing on every take.
