Each shot in the Breakdown now says three things in plain words: what we see, how to shoot it (where the camera goes and how long to record), and what it's for. Shots name the moments they play under, with their times, and each setup's notes read in full.

## Improvements

- **Signing in.** Direction checks your editor's sign-in properly. When it can't tell, it says why and you can check again, and you can always start anyway.
- **Video links just work.** Direction brings its own video downloader and keeps it up to date, so a YouTube link works without installing anything.
- **Fewer surprises on a new Mac.** No more prompt to install Apple's developer tools. Opened from the disk image, Direction offers to move itself to Applications. An editor that needs updating says how.
