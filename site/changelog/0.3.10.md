**Write a voice-over** from the Script room, the Film menu or the command menu. Say what it should say, and leave **Watch Cut 4 first** on. The editor watches that cut, then writes the words line by line. Each line is timed to the picture under it and stays clear of anyone already talking. The sheet tells you roughly how many words fit, so the picture has room to breathe.

The lines land in the Script room under **On screen**, **Voice-over** and **At**. Change any line and it stays yours, or leave a note and turn it over. When it reads right, **Copy for Recording** puts the lines on your clipboard with their times, ready to read from. It runs on the quicker editor.

**The Script room shows up for films you start from footage.** Bring in a script or story, or have one written from your takes, and you get a breakdown like any other film.
