# Sample film used in every frame

All frames show the same film so states line up. Use this data in previews and tests too.

**Film:** Launch film. A 3–4 minute documentary-style launch film about Maya, who spent two years building an editing tool.

**Brief**
- Logline: "Maya spent two years building the part nobody wanted to build."
- How it should feel: "Tired, then proud. Quiet, not triumphant. Let her face do the work."
- Length: 3:00–4:00. Audience: people who make things. Must keep: "Editing was the part nobody wanted." and the final shot of the empty office.
- References: *Jiro Dreams of Sushi* opening (pace), "a still of window light" (look). Avoid: stock music swells.
