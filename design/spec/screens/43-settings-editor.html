How much freedom the editor has
Do what I wrote

Changes only what each note names. Never offers options. Asks when a note is unclear.

Interprets what you meant. May touch the shots around a note. Cuts Feeling notes two ways.

Take a pass

May also suggest changes no note asked for. Each one is marked Unasked and waits for your OK.
