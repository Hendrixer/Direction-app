## Chrome Cut

File: [01-chrome-cut.png](01-chrome-cut.png)

### Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: standalone premium macOS app icon for Director, a native video editor used by a human director and AI agents.
Primary request: create ONE striking, original sculptural app icon. The user's inspiration is a dark macOS tile containing a luminous blue/violet glass object and another dark tile with vertically ribbed glass over a chrome abstract mark. Capture the luxurious material feel and visual boldness, but create an original Director identity.
Composition/framing: square 1:1 artwork. One large centered rounded-square/squircle app tile, front-facing, with subtly beveled black graphite edges and very small outer margin, against a uniform charcoal backdrop. Center a single bold emblem occupying approximately 65% of tile width, with balanced breathing room. The emblem must have a simple recognizable silhouette that survives downscaling to 32px. Polished high-end 3D industrial design, precise surfaces and optical reflections, restrained studio lighting.
Constraints: one icon only, no collage, no layout sheet, no text, no letters except the deliberately sculpted D emblem if called for, no wordmark, no labels, no watermark. No screenshot UI. Avoid film clapperboards, filmstrip perforations, tiny details, generic camera illustrations, rainbow gradients, excessive particles, and cheap neon gaming style.

Subject: a sculptural capital D made from ONE thick uninterrupted liquid-chrome band, with a confidently straight vertical left edge and a broad rounded right bowl. A single precise diagonal cut through the lower-right curve creates a clean small gap, expressing an editing cut without disrupting the D silhouette. Deep black spacious D-shaped counter. The band has a rounded rectangular cross-section, softly inflated surfaces, bold and minimal.
Materials/color: polished dark titanium and mirror silver with razor-sharp white highlight gradients; a small concentrated electric cobalt-blue reflection visible inside the diagonal cut and faint violet reflections along the bottom. The dark tile surface is smoked glass with very fine vertical fluting, subtle enough to leave the D clear and prominent. Silver and blue dominate. Strong tangible depth, sleek and slightly enigmatic. This is an elegant D monogram, not a horseshoe, not a play button.
